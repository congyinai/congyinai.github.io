Congyi Nai

My research focuses on predicting weather and climate with generative models. I aim to combine the experience accumulated by numerical weather prediction with the scaling laws of AI, toward a scalable paradigm for data-driven weather and climate prediction.

PhD student, Intelligent Earth, University of Oxford
